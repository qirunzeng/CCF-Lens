## Short description

Show CCF 2026, ICORE 2026, and TH-CPL 2019 ranks on Google Scholar, ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and DBLP.

## Detailed description

VenueRank Lens adds clear CCF A/B/C, CORE A*/A/B/C, and TH-CPL A/B badges beside publication venues while you browse Google Scholar, ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and DBLP.

Created by Qirun Zeng and developed openly on GitHub.

Highlights:

- Uses the official seventh-edition CCF 2026 catalog.
- Uses the official ICORE 2026 conference ranking. ICORE does not rank journals, so CORE badges appear only for conferences.
- Uses the official TH-CPL list approved in 2019, covering both conferences and journals. The year is stated explicitly because this is not a new 2026 ranking.
- Recognizes full venue names, common abbreviations, and safely truncated Google Scholar venue text.
- Distinguishes full conference papers from Findings, workshops, short papers, demos, technical briefs, summaries, and companion proceedings.
- Performs all matching locally in your browser.
- Contains no advertising, analytics, account system, or remote code.

VenueRank Lens is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the China Computer Federation, ICORE/CORE, or Tsinghua University.

## Single purpose

Display CCF 2026, ICORE 2026, and TH-CPL 2019 ranks beside publication venues on supported academic websites.

## Permission justification

VenueRank Lens requests access only to Google Scholar, ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and DBLP. It needs this host access to read publication venue text and insert the corresponding local CCF, CORE, and TH-CPL rank badges. No page data leaves the browser.

## Data-use declaration

The extension does not collect, store, sell, or transmit user data. It does not use analytics or tracking. All venue matching is performed locally against the catalog bundled with the extension.
