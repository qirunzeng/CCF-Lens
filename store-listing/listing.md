## Short description

Show CCF 2026 venue ranks directly on Google Scholar, ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and DBLP.

## Detailed description

CCF Lens adds clear CCF A, B, and C badges beside publication venues while you browse Google Scholar, ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and DBLP.

Created by Qirun Zeng and developed openly on GitHub.

Highlights:

- Uses the official seventh-edition CCF 2026 catalog.
- Recognizes full venue names, common abbreviations, and safely truncated Google Scholar venue text.
- Distinguishes full conference papers from Findings, workshops, short papers, demos, technical briefs, summaries, and companion proceedings.
- Performs all matching locally in your browser.
- Contains no advertising, analytics, account system, or remote code.

CCF Lens is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the China Computer Federation.

## Single purpose

Display the CCF 2026 recommendation rank beside publication venues on supported academic websites.

## Permission justification

CCF Lens requests access only to Google Scholar, ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and DBLP. It needs this host access to read publication venue text and insert the corresponding local CCF rank badge. No page data leaves the browser.

## Data-use declaration

The extension does not collect, store, sell, or transmit user data. It does not use analytics or tracking. All venue matching is performed locally against the catalog bundled with the extension.
