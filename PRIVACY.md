# CCF Lens Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

CCF Lens does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information or browsing data.

The extension reads publication venue text on supported pages only to match it against the bundled CCF 2026 catalog and display a rank badge. All processing happens locally in the browser. CCF Lens has no analytics, advertising, account system, remote code, or network service.

The extension runs only on these sites:

- Google Scholar (`scholar.google.com`)
- ACM Digital Library (`dl.acm.org`)
- IEEE Xplore (`ieeexplore.ieee.org`)
- DBLP (`dblp.org` and `dblp.uni-trier.de`)

Questions or reports can be submitted through the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/qirunzeng/CCF-Lens/issues).
